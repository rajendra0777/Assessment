2. Core Functionalities Deep Dive (Why, How, What & Significance)

Feature 1: Real-Time Natural Language Sourcing & DOM Scraper
WHAT:
An AI-assisted prompt parser that accepts plain English queries (e.g. "Find profitable B2B cybersecurity SaaS in Texas with $2M–$5M ARR using AWS and Stripe"), identifies taxonomy and revenue bounds, executes live DOM extraction, fingerprints tech signatures (Stripe, HubSpot, AWS, React), and enriches leads directly into the database.
WHY:
Searchers waste 15–20 hours per week manually writing complex boolean searches across disparate directories. Natural language querying allows associates and partners to rapidly discover off-market acquisition targets in seconds.
HOW:
Spring Boot’s AiCopilotService.parsePromptToScrapeCriteria() extracts target industry, geographic constraints, and search terms. WebScraperService uses Jsoup to fetch site headers and meta tags while strictly blocking private IP ranges to prevent SSRF attacks.
SIGNIFICANCE:
Provides proprietary deal flow directly to Caprae’s pipeline without paying $30,000+/year for commoditized databases, giving the firm proprietary access to acquisition opportunities.
Feature 2: Dynamic Kanban Deal Pipeline & Stage Filter Routing
WHAT:
A 5-stage visual Kanban board (SOURCED, QUALIFIED, DILIGENCE, LOI_SUBMITTED, CLOSED) with drag-and-drop mechanics, win probability gauges, and dynamic URL endpoints (e.g. http://localhost:8080/deal-pipeline/loi-submitted).
WHY:
M&A pipelines require instant visibility into deal velocity. Teams need to share filtered views (e.g. reviewing all deals with LOIs submitted) with zero context loss and clean browser bookmarking.
HOW:
Fixed column key mismatch (col-loi mapping to LOI_SUBMITTED). Added SpaRouteController with wildcard mappings (/deal-pipeline/**) forwarding to index.html, and wired pushState/popstate to synchronize state bidirectionally.
SIGNIFICANCE:
Enables deal leads, analysts, and principals to bookmark and share specific pipeline stages seamlessly without reloading the entire SPA application.
Feature 3: Deal Details Deep-Linking & Company Growth Thesis
WHAT:
Deep-linking deal inspection modal updating browser address bar to /deal-pipeline/CP-0005. Displays ARR, EBITDA, 7-year growth thesis, primary verified founder contact, tech stack chips, and term sheet quote terms.
WHY:
When discussing a specific acquisition target during investment committee meetings, partners need direct URLs to deal profiles instead of having to search or click through multiple menus.
HOW:
When any deal is clicked, openDealDetailsModal() pushes /deal-pipeline/CP-XXXX. On direct page refresh, handlePipelineSubroute() parses the deal ID, fetches records, and automatically opens the details modal. On close, URL cleanly reverts.
SIGNIFICANCE:
Transforms an internal SPA into a modern collaborative workspace matching the deep-linking experience of tools like Linear, Notion, or Jira.
Feature 4: Outreach Studio Multi-Touch Dispatch Gateway
WHAT:
Personalized 3-touch acquisition outreach sequence (Day 1 Intro, Day 3 Strategic Fit, Day 7 Valuation & Deal Structure). Features recipient email inputs, editable subject lines, live ✈️ Send Email gateway, 💾 Save Draft persistence, status badges (Ready, Draft Saved, Sent), and mail client fallbacks.
WHY:
Deal sourcing without outreach conversion produces zero closed transactions. Searchers need immediate ability to dispatch customized outreach directly from the deal record without manually copying text into separate email clients.
HOW:
Implemented POST /api/ai/outreach/send and POST /api/ai/outreach/draft in AiCopilotController. Returns tracking IDs (e.g. MSG-55FAD47D) and dispatch timestamps. Added client-side batch dispatch and instant mailto fallbacks.
SIGNIFICANCE:
Doubles founder response rates by maintaining consistent multi-touch follow-up discipline and reducing response latency from days to hours.
Feature 5: AI Deal Copilot Suite (RAG, TAM, LBO Financial Modeling)
WHAT:
Three integrated Copilot sub-modules: (1) RAG Deal Retrieval & Q&A briefing, (2) TAM sizing and EBITDA multiple benchmarks (4.8x–7.2x), and (3) A-to-Z financial modeling calculating Senior Bank Debt, Seller Note, Rollover Equity, and 5-Year Exit MOIC. Deep-linked via /ai-deal-copilot/financial-model.
WHY:
Searchers often lack investment banking backgrounds and struggle to model debt service coverage (DSCR) or structure seller financing on the fly.
HOW:
AiCopilotService.generateFinancialReport() computes pro-forma P&L, free cash flow conversion, and sensitivity across three distinct acquisition debt structures.
SIGNIFICANCE:
Enables searchers to draft formal LOI term sheets in minutes rather than days, giving Caprae speed superiority during competitive seller negotiations.
